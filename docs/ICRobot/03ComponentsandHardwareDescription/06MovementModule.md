# Movement Module
## Introduction
<font style="color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9);">The ICRobot robot uses a track drive system with encoded motors to provide power, ensuring high precision and stability in movement and steering. The track design has good grip and excellent passing ability. This type of drive allows the robot to complete tasks stably and flexibly in a variety of scenarios.</font>

## Usage Instructions
1. The motor is equipped with blocking protection function, it will stop when it detects abnormal motion status.

2. Two-side motor linkage control, if one side of the track is blocked, the other side of the motor will also stop.
