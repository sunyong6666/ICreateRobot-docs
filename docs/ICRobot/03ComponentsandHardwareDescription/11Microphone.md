## Introduction
The robot is equipped with two miniature high-performance silicon microphones that feature low power consumption and are suitable for long-term sound collection. They offer low noise, high signal-to-noise ratio, and wide frequency response, enabling precise capture of full-spectrum audio and efficient conversion into electrical signals to ensure accurate and complete sound data. This design enhances spatial sound perception, making it ideal for various applications such as voice recognition and environmental sound monitoring, meeting diverse user needs.

## Usage Instructions
ICRobot robot combined with microphone can achieve a variety of application scenarios, such as: detecting the sound size, intelligent voice interaction and so on.
